Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Plain summary. We collect the account details you give us, the trade journal content you create, and basic technical logs. We use them to run and secure the Service. We do not sell your data, we do not use your journal for advertising, and you can ask for a copy or deletion at any time.

1. Who we are

QuantumEdge Alpha ("we", "us") is the controller of personal data processed through www.quantumedgealpha.com and the QuantumEdge Alpha application. Privacy contact: privacy@quantumedgealpha.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, hashed authentication credentials, and subscription or billing identifiers.
  • Billing: handled by our payment processor. Card numbers never reach our servers; we receive only a token, plan, and status.
  • Content you create: trade journal entries, notes, saved layouts, watchlists, and any reflections or emotional-state markers you choose to record.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, browser, operating system, referring page, and timestamps.
  • Usage telemetry: which features are opened, session duration, error events, and performance diagnostics.

3. A note on sensitive inference

A trade journal, combined with emotional-state or wellbeing markers, can reveal financial position and, indirectly, information about health or behaviour. We treat all journal content as confidential. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, do not share it with data brokers, and do not use it to build behavioural profiles for anyone other than you, for the sole purpose of showing you your own analytics.

4. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or comparable law applies we rely on: performance of a contract (delivering the Service); legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, product diagnostics, defending legal claims); consent (marketing email, optional analytics cookies); and legal obligation (tax, accounting, and lawful requests).

5. Processors and sub-processors

We use a small set of vetted providers, each bound by contract to process data only on our instructions: Supabase (database and authentication), our payment processor (billing), Cloudflare and GitHub Pages (hosting, CDN, and security), and email delivery providers for transactional mail. We do not send your display name or email to third-party avatar services.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where data leaves the EEA or UK we rely on an adequacy decision or on Standard Contractual Clauses plus supplementary technical measures such as encryption in transit and at rest.

7. Retention

Account and journal data are retained while your account is open and for up to 30 days after closure in backups. Billing records are retained as long as tax law requires. Technical logs are retained for up to 90 days. Aggregated, de-identified diagnostics may be kept indefinitely.

8. Your rights

Subject to local law you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or object to processing, and you may withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing. Email privacy@quantumedgealpha.com and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), hashed credentials, least-privilege access, and audit logging. No system is impenetrable; if a breach affects your rights we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

10. Cookies and local storage

The marketing site sets no advertising cookies. The application stores two items in your browser's localStorage — qe_user_profile (your display preferences) and qe_user_trades (your journal, so a refresh does not lose work). Both are strictly functional, stay on your device, and are cleared when you sign out or clear site data. We use no third-party trackers on this site.

11. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

If we make a material change to this policy we will post the updated version here, change the date above, and — where the change materially affects your rights — notify you by email before it takes effect.
