Does the platform place trades or hold my money?

No. QuantumEdge Alpha never executes, routes, clears, or settles orders, and never holds customer funds or securities. You keep your own brokerage account and place every order yourself.

What do GEX, Vanna, and Charm actually measure?

They are model estimates of dealer Greek exposure built from third-party options chains and assumptions about dealer positioning — most commonly that dealers are short gamma in aggregate. They describe a model of positioning, not a level the market is obliged to respect, and they can be wrong at exactly the moments that matter, such as large expiries.

Why are the numbers I see different from my broker's?

Market data may be delayed, sampled, or revised, and exposure estimates depend on assumptions about open interest and dealer hedging. Treat platform figures as a second opinion and verify against your broker before you act.

Are the trades and P&L on this site real?

No. This is a demonstration build: accounts, positions, trades, scanner candidates, and performance figures are synthetic and generated for illustration. Hypothetical results do not reflect real liquidity, fills, or costs and are not indicative of future results.

How is position size calculated?

The sizing module illustrates Kelly-criterion and fractional-Kelly methods. Its output depends entirely on the win-rate and payoff inputs supplied, so it is shown to explain a method — not to recommend risking a specific percentage of your capital.
